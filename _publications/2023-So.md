A study of the properties of the soil layer in Davao City, the Philippines, using the Multi-channel Analysis of Surface Waves (MASW) and seismic refraction techniques. The aims of the study are to generate shear-wave and primary-wave velocity models, and to obtain a Vs30 contour map. Both the 2D Vs and Vp models can be divided into three layers based on borehole data. The top layer is related to a clay layer and a sand-clay and/or sand-silt layer. The second layer corresponds to gravels with a clay layer. The bottom layer corresponds to volcanic breccia. The Vs30 contour map is derived from MASW (WaveEq), estimated Vs30 (Wang & Wang, 2015), seismic refraction, and lithologic logs.
