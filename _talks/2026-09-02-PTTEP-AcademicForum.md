An invited presentation at PTTEP's annual Academic Forum, part of the *Subsurface Data for U*
program, through which PTTEP and its industry partners give universities geoscience data,
software and hardware for teaching and research.

I showed how the Geoscience Division at Mahidol University, Kanchanaburi Campus puts the
program's data to work:

- **Courses** — Petroleum Geophysics (KAGS478/KAGS491) taught on industry software and real subsurface data.
- **Senior projects** — undergraduate studies built on the data, including a rock-physics feasibility study for CO₂ storage using fluid substitution, and a 3-D seismic interpretation and structural model of an onshore Thai basin.
