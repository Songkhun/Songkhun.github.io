Took part in the **Kiro training and hands-on Kiro Mini Hackathon** on 8–9 October 2026, part of the *Mahidol AI Community 2026* programme, *AI Adoption Challenge with Kiro*, Track A: faculty and staff. The event was run by the Information Technology Division, Mahidol University.

The training introduced Kiro, an AI coding environment, followed by a short hackathon building with it. I went on to use Kiro for [Kanchanaburi GeoGuard](/portfolio/2026-10-17-GeoGuard/), my entry to the AI Development Challenge.
