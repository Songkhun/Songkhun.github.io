A dark-mode typing trainer I built to get faster at typing in both English and Thai. It runs in
the browser, or as a Mac app with its own Dock icon.

[View the project on GitHub](https://github.com/Songkhun/TypeRush)

### Three ways to practise

- **Test**: 15, 30 or 60 second runs, or 10, 25 or 50 words, with live WPM, a combo counter and
  an on-screen keyboard that lights up the next key
- **Drill**: the app records which keys you miss or hesitate on, then builds word lists around
  your five weakest keys
- **Arcade (Word Defense)**: type the falling words before they reach your ship; waves speed up,
  every fifth wave sends a boss phrase, and clean streaks raise a ×4 multiplier

### Built for Thai as well as English

- **One keystroke, one character.** Thai vowels and tone marks are typed as separate keys, so the
  app checks every code point while still drawing whole syllables on screen.
- **Kedmanee keyboard on screen.** The next key lights up, including Shift for shifted characters.
- **Wrong-keyboard warning.** Three English letters in a Thai test bring up a reminder to press
  ⌃Space and switch the Mac keyboard.
- **Fonts that show Thai properly.** Each of the five font choices is paired with a Thai font.

### Keeping it fun

XP and levels, a daily goal with a 🔥 streak, personal-best confetti, four key-sound packs and
five colour themes. Progress stays in the browser on your own computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

### How it is built

- **Logic separate from the screen.** Scoring, weak-key tracking, XP and the arcade simulation are
  plain TypeScript with no React or browser code, covered by 114 unit tests.
- **Standard definitions.** WPM is correct characters ÷ 5 per minute, the same rule typing sites
  use, so results can be compared.
- **A usage diagram in the repository.** A draw.io map shows how to run the app, move between
  its screens, and change its options.
