About me
--------
I am an Assistant Professor and Head of the Geoscience Division at Mahidol University, Kanchanaburi Campus, Thailand, where I have taught since 2012.
I teach geophysics, petroleum geophysics, Python programming and data science. During my Master’s degree, I developed a forward modeling and inversion program for Direct Current (DC) Resistivity data by using MATLAB, resulting in a publication (Boonchaisuk et al., 2008). 
For my Ph.D., I led a survey team to study the crustal structure of Kanchanaburi Province using magnetotelluric and receiver function techniques, leading to Thailand’s first publication in this field (Boonchaisuk et al., 2013). 
My work involved installing seismic stations, data acquisition, data processing, and inversion, which marked the beginning of my interest in seismology. 

After graduation, I contributed to the study of Thailand’s crustal properties and thickness using receiver function analysis (Noisagool et al., 2014). Following the largest earthquake in Thai history on May 5, 2014, I was involved in conducting a magnetotelluric survey and proposed a model related to the earthquake’s cause (Boonchaisuk et al., 2017). 

Current work
------------
- **The Three Pagodas Fault.** I lead an NRCT-funded project (2026–2027) that images this active fault in western Thailand with broadband magnetotelluric profiles, combined with gravity and magnetic data. The aim is to measure the dip, depth and fluid content of a fault that hazard models still draw as a vertical plane.
- **Machine learning for geoscience.** Since the [SIP2025 program at KIGAM](/portfolio/2025-09-20-SIP2025/), I have been working on turning resistivity models into geotechnical parameters and on extracting fractures and lineaments from satellite imagery, the latter in collaboration with PTTEP.
- **Software for teaching.** I write small, open tools that let students see the physics they compute: [gravity forward modelling](/portfolio/2026-09-01-Gravis/), [resistivity inversion](/portfolio/2026-10-04-InversionCoach/), [3-D magnetotelluric models](/portfolio/2026-09-23-MTViewer/) and [machine learning](/portfolio/2026-09-11-MLdemo/). See the [portfolio](/portfolio/) for the full list.

You can browse my [publications](/publications/), the [courses I teach](/teaching/), the [code I share](/software/), or my full [CV](/cv/).

Research interests
------------------
Magnetotellurics and active-fault imaging, seismology and earthquake hazard in Thailand, and the application of machine learning to geophysics and geology. I developed and teach Introduction to Python (KAGS381) and Introduction to Data Science (KAGS383) for our students, and I am working towards a Data Science track for Geoscience students and other divisions at our campus.
