Earthquake section (weeks 12–15) of KAED343: magnitude, location, frequency content, ground-motion intensity, recurrence and mitigation, worked from real data recorded at Thai stations.

**Course website (Thai): [songkhun.github.io/courses/kaed343/](/courses/kaed343/)** with lessons, lab step cards, quizzes, a formula cheat sheet and downloads.

Topics
------

- Earthquake magnitude (ML, mb, Ms, Mw) and locating earthquakes
- Seismic wave frequency, noise and power spectra
- Ground-motion intensity, GMPEs and the Modified Mercalli scale
- Gutenberg–Richter recurrence and earthquake probability
- Fault-size scaling and maximum magnitude
- Building resonance and mitigation
