Basic principles of geophysics applied to the study of subsurface geology: gravity, magnetic, electric fields and seismic methods in terms of data processing and interpretation; teamwork.

Taught 2022–2024 (1 credit laboratory, core course, Bachelor of Science in Geoscience), alongside [KAGS351 Geophysics](/teaching/2024-KAGS351).

Topics
------

- Survey planning and case studies
- DC resistivity acquisition, processing (RES1D, IPI2Win) and 2-D interpretation (RES2DINV)
- Gravity processing, forward modelling in Excel and interpretation
- Seismic refraction acquisition, picking (PickWin), processing (PlotRefa) and interpretation
- Seismic reflection: faults and lineaments
- Magnetic and paleomagnetic interpretation
- GPR acquisition
