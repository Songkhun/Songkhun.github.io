Basic principles of geophysics. Application of geophysics to the study of subsurface geology: DC resistivity, gravity, magnetic, electric fields, and seismic methods; self-responsibility.

Taught 2021–2024 (2 credits, core course, Bachelor of Science in Geoscience). Replaced by [KAGS356 Geophysics](/teaching/2025-KAGS356) in 2025.

Topics
------

- Introduction
- Survey planning
- DC resistivity
- Gravity
- Seismic refraction
- Seismic reflection
- Magnetic and paleomagnetic methods
- Ground-penetrating radar (GPR)
- Report and student presentation
