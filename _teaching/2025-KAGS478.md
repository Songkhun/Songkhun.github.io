Integrated geophysical methods for petroleum exploration. Fundamentals of subsurface imaging by multi-channel seismic reflection techniques as used in oil exploration. Covers survey design, acquisition, processing, and interpretation in both 2-D and 3-D seismic reflection data and wire-line logging.

Topics
------

- Introduction
- Seismic Data Acquisition
- Seismic Data Processing
- Structural and Stratigraphic Interpretation
