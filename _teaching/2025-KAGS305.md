Natural hazards related to geology and their monitoring and mitigation: endogenic origin, earthquake hazards, volcanic eruption and exogenous origin; landslide, land subsidence, crust collapse, shoreline erosion, flooding, and groundwater contamination.

Taught 2022–2024 (2 credits, Bachelor of Science in Geoscience), co-taught with Dr. Patchawee Nualkhao, who covers landslides and slope stability. I teach the earthquake-hazard part.

Topics (earthquake hazard)
------

- Basic principles of earthquakes
- Seismic waves and the development of seismology
- Locating earthquakes and magnitude
- Active faults in Thailand
- Seismicity models
- Ground motion prediction (GMPEs)
- Basics of seismic hazard analysis
- Probabilistic seismic hazard analysis (PSHA)
