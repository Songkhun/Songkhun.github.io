Invited lecturer for the opening day of the M.Sc. Energy Geosciences geophysics course, academic year 2026/27.
The day is 30% theory and 70% hands-on, built around a hypothetical CO₂ storage site.

Topics
------

- Rock–fluid physics: density, velocity, acoustic impedance and resistivity, with a brine-to-CO₂ fluid substitution exercise (Gassmann and Archie)
- The multi-physics toolbox: seismic, gravity, magnetic and electromagnetic methods, and choosing a method for a target
- Scale and resolution: what geophysical data can and cannot resolve
- Managing uncertainty in real energy projects
