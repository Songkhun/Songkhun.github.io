Elective, 3(3-0-6); prerequisite KAGS356 Geophysics. Course coordinator, first semester of academic year 2026.

Integrated geophysical methods for petroleum exploration: the fundamentals of subsurface imaging by multichannel seismic reflection,
survey design, acquisition, processing and interpretation of 2-D and 3-D seismic reflection data, and wireline logging.
The course draws on industry data and guest lectures from the PTTEP *Subsurface Data for U* program.
