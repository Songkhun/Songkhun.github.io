Geological hazards with a focus on earthquakes: how they happen, how they are measured, and how seismic hazard is estimated for engineering and planning. Successor to [KAGS305 Geohazards](/teaching/2025-KAGS305).

Topics
------

- Introduction to earthquakes
- Probability for hazard analysis
- Case studies: the 2014 Phan (Mae Lao) earthquake, the 2025 Myanmar earthquake and the 2025 Japan earthquake
- Fault rupture and magnitude scaling (Wells & Coppersmith)
- Seismicity models
- Ground motion prediction (GMPEs)
- Probabilistic seismic hazard analysis (PSHA), with MATLAB exercises
